Tutorial 1/2: Rules of the Target Interception Game

We are glad that you decided to join us in this study. Before starting the target interception game, please read the following descriptions and rules carefully.

What is the target interception game about?

In this game, you will team up with an AI to catch moving targets inside a circular field. The targets move in straight lines, but not all at the same speed: the more urgent a target is, the faster it moves (see “scoring rules” below), so urgent targets cross the circle and leave it sooner. Your job (as well as your AI teammate’s job) is simple: just pick a target to go after, and the game will automatically move you (or the AI) along the best path to catch it.

When a target is caught inside the circle, your team earns points no matter who catches it. In addition, the catcher may get extra bonus points (see “scoring rules” below), and need to pick the next target to go after.

Targets will disappear if they leave the circle before being caught, and new ones can appear at any place or any time. Each game lasts for 1 minute.

Scoring rules

Targets in the game vary by their urgency and shape, which leads to different points:

  • The urgency of a target is shown by how much of it is filled in - the more filled it is, the more urgent it is. There are three urgency levels, and each gives a different number of points to the team (i.e., you + the AI) when caught.
  • More urgent targets also move faster: a Level 3 target moves about twice as fast as a Level 1 target. Fast targets are worth more points, but they leave the circle more quickly, so you have less time to catch them.

Level 1 (least urgent) Slow +1 point for the team
Level 2 (moderately urgent) Medium +2 points for the team
Level 3 (most urgent) Fast +3 points for the team
  • The shape of a target determines the bonus points for the person who catches it:
    • You (the human player) get an extra 3 bonus points for each square target you catch. You do not get bonus points for catching circle or triangle targets.
    • The AI gets an extra 3 bonus points for each triangle target it catches. It does not get bonus points for catching circle or square targets.

By the end of a target interception game:

  • Your final score is the team's points (from all targets caught by either you or the AI) plus your bonus points (from the square targets you catch).
  • Your AI teammate's final score is the team's points (from all targets caught by either you or the AI) plus the AI's bonus points (from the triangle targets the AI catches).

What is your goal in a game?

In each game, your goal is to work together with the AI and choose targets wisely to get the highest score for yourself - this includes both the team's points and your personal bonus points. Your final score in a game will be converted to your bonus payments in this study using a 100 points to $0.5 rate.
